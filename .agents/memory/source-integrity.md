name: Source integrity and catalogue decisions
description: Why unavailable training facts are withheld and occupation references must not become Malaysian labour-demand claims.
---

Do not restore seeded course prices, durations or claimability as live facts merely because a provider URL opens. Until a provider feed verifies those attributes, show unavailable rather than free or zero. Preserve historical plans but withhold unverified totals.

**Why:** The source audit found link reachability had been treated as evidence for unrelated training facts. The user requires genuine sources and explicit missing-data states, not plausible sample values.

**How to apply:** Require attribute-level provenance before enabling course recommendations, prices or HRD Corp claims.

O*NET is a US occupation reference, not Malaysian vacancies or MyCOL certification. An application SOC crosswalk is not an official MASCO equivalence. AI task exposure is not labour demand.

**Why:** Related-looking classifications and AI usage metrics cannot substantiate Malaysian demand claims.

**How to apply:** Keep geography and mapping limitations visible, and leave demand/MyCOL unknown without a verified Malaysian source.

Review O*NET file layout before a version upgrade: version 31 separated essential/transferable skills and renamed the interest files.

**Why:** Assuming older Skills/Interests filenames fails against the new official archive.

**How to apply:** Inspect the official archive and publisher's schema; never silently fall back to editorial role-skill weights.
